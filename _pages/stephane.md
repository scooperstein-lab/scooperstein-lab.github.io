<div class="person-name"><strong>Stephane Cooperstein</strong></div>
<div class="person-role"><em>Principal Investigator</em></div>

Stephane leads the Cooperstein Lab at Texas A&M University as its Principal Investigator. He earned his PhD at Princeton University and was a postdoctoral researcher at UC San Diego (UCSD). The group works on the CMS Experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), studying Higgs boson pair production in the four-bottom-quark final state ($$\mathrm{HH} \to 4\mathrm{b}$$) and the Higgs decay to muons ($$\mathrm{H} \to \mu^+\mu^-$$).
