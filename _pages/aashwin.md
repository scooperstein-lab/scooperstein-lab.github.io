I am a postdoctoral researcher, and I have been with the Cooperstein group since August 2025. Originally from Nepal, I earned my PhD at The Ohio State University (Go Bucks!). My research focuses on constraining the Higgs self-coupling through Higgs boson pair production in the four-bottom-quark final state, using the fully merged topology. For my PhD, I worked on searches for new physics in the top quark sector using the framework of effective field theory (EFT). In my free time, I enjoy cooking, playing tennis, and watching soccer.
